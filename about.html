About MedBill

Medical billing is the largest consumer financial market in the United States — and the most error-prone. 30-50% of all medical bills contain overcharges. MedBill exists to level the playing field.

Our Mission

We believe that no one should pay for a medical error they didn't cause. Our mission is to make professional-grade medical bill auditing accessible to every patient — not just those who can afford a human advocate charging 20-35% of savings.

Using AI-powered analysis combined with CMS rate data and CPT coding rules, MedBill detects billing errors in minutes, not weeks. We put the tools of a professional billing advocate into every patient's hands for a flat $29.

💰 $750B+ Problem

Medical billing waste costs the US healthcare system over $750 billion annually. Administrative complexity is the primary driver, and patients bear the cost.

⚡ 2 Minutes vs 2 Weeks

Traditional billing advocates take 2-6 weeks and charge 20-35% of savings. MedBill delivers results in under 5 minutes for a flat fee.

🔒 Privacy First

Your medical bill is encrypted in transit and at rest. Processed in memory. Never shared or sold. Deleted within 24 hours.

📊 Data-Driven

Powered by the CMS Physician Fee Schedule (1M+ rates), CPT coding guidelines, and AI pattern recognition — not guesswork.

Our Approach

MedBill is built on a hybrid architecture:

  • Deterministic Engine: CMS rate comparison across 50+ common CPT codes (physician fee schedule, facility/non-facility pricing, geographic adjustments)
  • AI Audit Overlay: LLM-powered detection of upcoding, unbundling, duplicate charges, modifier errors, balance billing, and denial code errors
  • Autonomous Pipeline: Submit and forget — our cron-based processing delivers results via email while you go about your day
  • Actionable Outputs: Not just findings — a ready-to-send dispute letter, phone negotiation script, and CMS rate comparison table

Who We Serve

  • Consumers: Patients who received a hospital bill, ER visit bill, surgical bill, lab bill, or Explanation of Benefits and want to know if they're being overcharged.
  • Medical Practices: Small to mid-size practices, clinics, and nursing homes that need bulk audit processing, compliance checks, and denial rate reduction.
  • Billing Departments: Teams that want automated quality control on every bill before it goes out the door.
